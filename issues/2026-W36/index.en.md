Official 2026–2030 planning targets wind-plus-solar above 2,800 GW by 2030 with renewables at 30% of generation. Reports say Musk cited China's build speed at a G20 ministerial to argue US data centers must find "power sources outside China." China's next exam is absorption, transmission and tariffs — not installed capacity.
