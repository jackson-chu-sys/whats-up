## ▶ Ebola in the DRC: No Approved Vaccine for This Strain, So Ervebo Goes Into a Trial

This outbreak is caused by the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola, for which there is no licensed vaccine and no specific treatment. ## ▶ Optogenetics Moves From the Nobel to the Bedside: 6 of 10 Patients Regain Light Perception

On October 8 the New England Journal of Medicine published PIONEER phase 1/2 results for GS030: ten patients with advanced retinitis pigmentosa received an AAV vector carrying ChrimsonR in their worse eye, then wore goggles that convert scenes into amber light pulses, bypassing dead photoreceptors to activate surviving retinal ganglion cells. Seven patients improved light sensitivity by 2.0 to 62.3 times, six crossed the prespecified clinical threshold, and some could locate and touch objects; the 34 ocular adverse events were mostly mild to moderate. Days before publication, optogenetics won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.
