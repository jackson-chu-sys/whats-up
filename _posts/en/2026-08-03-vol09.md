## ▶ An Open-Source Answer to the Heat Dome: CityShade Proposes "Retractable Solar Shading" as a New Urban Cooling Paradigm

As multiple Northern Hemisphere cities bake under heat domes, a technical white paper from a Chinese team offers an ambitious answer: **intercept the sunlight heating cities and convert it directly into electricity**. The CityShade project proposes a "distributed retractable solar shading system" — deploying foldable solar shading devices over parking lots, sun-facing building facades, and urban leisure spaces to achieve a triple effect: blocking radiant heat gain, actively generating power, and adding no burden to the grid. The white paper's estimates show a parking-lot scenario (16.5 kWp) generating ~27,375 kWh per year with a payback period of ~3.8 years; a building-facade scenario cutting ~63 tons of CO₂ per year. As global heat waves become the norm and air conditioning amplifies urban heat islands through feedback loops, this approach of "turning the problem itself into energy" deserves attention.
