NVIDIA launched Nemotron-Labs-TwoTower in late July — an open-weight diffusion language model (Diffusion LLM) employing parallel text generation rather than traditional token-by-token autoregressive decoding. ## ▶ WHO Issues New Guidelines on Cognitive Decline and Dementia Prevention: 12 Evidence-Based Recommendations

The World Health Organization released updated guidelines this week on reducing the risk of cognitive decline and dementia, outlining 12 evidence-based intervention recommendations. Key takeaways: controlling hypertension, diabetes, and hearing loss are the three most impactful modifiable risk factors for dementia prevention; increased physical activity, Mediterranean diet adherence, and social engagement all significantly reduce risk; supplements such as vitamin E or ginkgo biloba are not recommended for dementia prevention. An estimated 55 million people worldwide live with dementia, with nearly 10 million new cases annually. WHO calls on nations to integrate brain health into universal health coverage frameworks.

## ▶ AI Medical Imaging Breakthrough: Multimodal Model Surpasses Radiologists in Lung Cancer Screening

A multicenter study published in *Nature Medicine* demonstrates that a multimodal AI model combining vision and text achieved 94.7% diagnostic accuracy in low-dose CT lung cancer screening, surpassing the average performance of participating senior radiologists (91.2%). The model simultaneously analyzes CT images, patient history, and genetic risk scores, delivering a risk assessment report within 30 seconds. The research team spans MIT, Stanford, and Johns Hopkins and has completed prospective validation across 12 hospitals. Reviewers caution, however, that issues of data bias, model explainability, and liability attribution must be addressed before real-world deployment.
